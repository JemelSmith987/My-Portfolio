Client Guidelines

Studio Policies

Please review our studio terms to ensure a seamless experience for all clients.

Deposits

  • A £15 non-refundable deposit is required to secure all appointments.
  • Appointments are not confirmed until the deposit has been received.
  • Deposits will be deducted from your final balance on the appointment day.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • A minimum of 48 hours' notice is required for any rescheduling requests.
  • Deposits remain strictly non-refundable.
  • Same-day cancellations and no-shows will result in loss of deposit.

Late Arrivals

  • Please arrive on time for your scheduled appointment.
  • Clients arriving more than 15 minutes late may have their appointment shortened or canceled.
  • Late arrivals affect overall service quality as artistry cannot be rushed.

Appointment Preparation

  • Arrive with a clean, freshly moisturized face with no prior makeup.
  • Brows should be groomed and lips exfoliated where possible.
  • Time spent removing makeup will be deducted from your appointment time.

Allergies & Skin Conditions

  • Please notify us before your appointment of any skin sensitivities or allergies.
  • This includes recent use of retinol, acne treatments, or chemical skin peels.

Guests & Studio Space

  • Due to limited studio space, only the client receiving the service may attend.
  • No additional friends, children, or family members permitted.

Payment Methods: Remaining balances must be paid on the day of appointment. Bank Transfer and Cash are accepted.
